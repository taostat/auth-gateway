### What you must not do

- Use the Taostats name or logo to imply endorsement of your product or service
- Use the Taostats name or logo in a way that suggests your service IS Taostats
- Register any domain names or social media accounts using the Taostats name

### Removing branding

If you wish to remove Taostats branding from the login pages, you must also
remove all other Taostats trademarks (name, logo, favicon, OG images) from
your deployment. You may not selectively use the Taostats name while removing
the logo, or vice versa.
