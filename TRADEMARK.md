### What you may do

- Use this software freely under the Apache 2.0 license

- Deploy your own instance of the auth gateway

### What you must do

- **Attribution**: Include the NOTICE file and license in any redistribution,
  as required by the Apache 2.0 license
- **Remove Taostats branding**: If you operate an independent deployment
  (not operated by or on behalf of Taostats), you must remove all Taostats
  branding — name, logo, favicon, and Open Graph images — from user-facing
  authentication pages. Retaining Taostats branding misleads users into
  believing Taostats operates or endorses your service.

### What you must not do

- Use the Taostats name or logo to imply endorsement of your product or service
- Use the Taostats name or logo in a way that suggests your service IS Taostats
- Register any domain names or social media accounts using the Taostats name
- Selectively retain some Taostats branding (e.g. the name) while removing
  other elements (e.g. the logo)
